EDITORIAL

I report on politics and policy for AL.com, a Pulitzer-winning news outlet that covers the state of Alabama. You can read my bylines here.

I also edit stories and projects and write a weekly newsletter, Politics Plus, for AL.com.

I began my journalism career as a news reporter in Charlottesville, Virginia.

STRATEGY

In 2021, I founded The Alabama Education Lab, a grant-funded team within AL.com that focused on K-12 education news.

I have developed award-winning projects, products and events for AL.com.

OTHER WORK
